✨ What is JXT-Music-Widget?

JXT-Music-Widget is a free desktop application for Windows 10 and Windows 11 that sits on your screen and shows you exactly what's playing right now. Whether you use Spotify, a local media player, or any other app that supports media controls, this widget keeps you in the loop at all times. It's like having a mini music dashboard right on your desktop, always visible, always helpful.

You don't need to be a tech wizard to use it. If you can download a file and double-click it, you're good to go. No coding, no complicated settings, just a beautiful widget that works out of the box.

📝 Frequently Asked Questions

Is JXT-Music-Widget really free?

Yes! It's completely free to download and use. No hidden fees, no premium tiers, just a great music widget.

Will it work with my music app?

If your music app supports standard Windows media controls (play/pause/next/previous), JXT-Music-Widget will work with it. This includes popular apps like Spotify, Apple Music, and many others.

Can I move the widget anywhere on my screen?

Absolutely! Simply click and drag the widget to your preferred position on the desktop. It will stay where you put it.

Does it use a lot of CPU or battery?

No, it's designed to be lightweight and efficient. It runs quietly in the background and uses minimal system resources.

🚦 Troubleshooting Tips

Having trouble? Here are some common fixes:

  • Widget not showing music: Make sure your music app is playing and supports media controls.
  • Widget not starting: Check that you have .NET Framework installed (Windows usually does this automatically).
  • Can't find the download: Make sure you're looking at the latest release on the GitHub releases page.
  • Theme not applying: Try restarting the widget after changing themes.
