I am a postdoctoral researcher in theoretical physics at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/) and the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/).

I obtained my PhD in 2026 at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en), advised by [Angelo Esposito](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1233187) and [Alfredo Urbano](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1060008).
My research is in physics beyond the Standard Model. I work on the QCD axion, from the quality of its potential to new ways of detecting it as dark matter; on naturalness and the hierarchy problem; and on effective field theories for systems that spontaneously break spacetime symmetries, which connect particle physics with condensed matter. I am also interested in cosmology, and in particular in the large-scale structure of the Universe, where effective field theory methods play a central role.
