I am a postdoctoral researcher in theoretical physics at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/) and the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/) where I work with the [group](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/particle/perez/) of [Gilad Perez](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1008388?ui-citation-summary=true), [Csaba Csaki](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1012738?ui-citation-summary=true) and [Yuval Grossman](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1007437).

I obtained my PhD in 2026 at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en), where I was advised by [Angelo Esposito](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1233187) and [Alfredo Urbano](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1060008) and also worked with [Roberto Contino](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1019481).

I work on the QCD axion, from the quality of its potential to new ways of detecting it as dark matter; on BSM theory and phenomenology; on effective field theories for systems that spontaneously break spacetime symmetries, which connect particle physics with condensed matter and cosmology.
